I am an Applied Scientist-II at Amazon, researching safety alignment, low-latency guardrails, and foundation model inference for Alexa+. My work focuses on training and distilling compact decoder-based safety classifiers (such as Qwen3-Guard, PRISM, and SAGE) capable of real-time policy enforcement with sub-40ms latency and high precision.

Previously, I was a Data Scientist at Ola Krutrim, where I led multimodal foundation model research. I built Chitrarth and Chitrarth-vl2—one of the earliest comprehensive Vision-Language Models for 22 Indian languages—spanning visual instruction tuning, cross-lingual OCR, video understanding, and the BharatBench evaluation suite.

I received my integrated Dual Degree (B.Tech & M.Tech) from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur (2013–2018). My overarching scientific goal is to bridge vision and language for linguistically diverse populations while ensuring generative systems remain provably aligned, robust, and safe in live human interactions.

Chitranuvad: Adapting Multilingual LLMs for Multimodal Translation

EMNLP '24

Investigates adapting multilingual generative LLMs for visually-conditioned translation. By grounding textual representations with co-occurring scene context, Chitranuvad eliminates lexical ambiguity and substantially outperforms text-only translation baselines across low-resource Indian language pairs.

Amazon Alexa+ Guardrails: PRISM & SAGE Frameworks

AMLC '26

Architected ultra low-latency (<40ms) decoder classification guard models (Qwen3-Guard) achieving 95% precision for live conversational foundation models. Developed PRISM (auxiliary head regularization) and SAGE (hierarchical prefill readout safety) to distill reasoning into fast runtime guardrails.

Research Themes & Focus Areas

Methodological Pillars

Multimodal VLMs

Visual instruction tuning, cross-lingual vision-language alignment, document OCR intelligence (Qwen2.5-VL, Phi-3.5), and CCTV video understanding (VideoLLaMA3).

Safety & Guardrails

Decoder safety distillation, runtime policy enforcement (<40ms latency), PRISM auxiliary head regularization, SAGE prefill readout, and automated synthetic policy agents.

Indic & Multilingual AI

Democratizing AI across 22 Indic languages, high-compression bilingual tokenization (SuperBPE), multimodal machine translation (Chitranuvad), and cultural benchmarks (BharatBench).

Agentic Systems

Model Context Protocol (MCP) agent orchestration, tool-augmented reasoning workflows, low-latency distributed serving, and continuous automated retraining pipelines.
